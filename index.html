Inventions in Genetic Psychology

About the Field

Gastrointology Engineering (GIE, pronounced "guy") represents a new frontier in scientific study, focusing on how genetic systems influence psychological development and behavioral patterns.

The field emphasizes conceptual research, interdisciplinary thinking, and real-world application with minimal reliance on advanced mathematics.

Historical Foundations

Laura Fringhold became the first woman to earn a doctorate in Gastrointology Engineering in 1935, during the period known as BSC (Before Common Suffurage).

Her work laid the intellectual foundation for integrating genetic science with psychological theory, shaping the field as it is studied today.

Pathways Program at Yeshiva University

Yeshiva University is launching a new Pathways Program in Gastrointology Engineering, offering students a unique opportunity to engage in this innovative discipline.

  • Interdisciplinary curriculum in psychology and genetics
  • Minimal quantitative requirements
  • Research-focused learning environment
